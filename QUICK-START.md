## ⚠️ Optional: Compress Images

Your images are large. Compress them for better performance:

```
Tools:
- TinyPNG.com (online)
- Squoosh.app (online)
- ImageOptim (Mac app)

Target sizes:
- Hero: 2.6MB → 300-500KB
- Products: 1.8MB → 200-300KB
- Services: 1.2MB → 150-250KB
- Video: 21.9MB → 2-5MB
```
